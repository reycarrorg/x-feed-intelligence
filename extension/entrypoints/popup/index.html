Scrolling is opt-in. XFI records only top-level cards that become at least 50% visible. Auto-scroll waits for the page to settle, moves at most 120 px per step, and stops when progress or safety checks fail. It never likes, follows, clicks, opens links, reads cookies, or calls X APIs.
Safety boundaries
  • Captured posts are untrusted data.
  • Maximum 10,000 visible cards, 8 hours, or 15 MiB; the first limit reached stops collection.
  • Collection pauses while the tab is hidden.
  • Login, challenge, rate-limit, and prompt-injection surfaces stop the session.
  • Export opens a Save dialog. Choose Documents there; XFI cannot force that folder or change your browser download setting.
  • After the eleventh completed, extension-owned export, the oldest verified extension-owned file is recycled. Renamed or unverified files are never deleted automatically.
