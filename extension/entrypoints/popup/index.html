Settings

Scrolling is opt-in. XFI records only top-level cards that become at least 50% visible. Hands-free scrolling is configured in Settings, stops when you touch the mouse or keyboard, and never moves the real pointer. It never likes, follows, clicks, opens links, reads cookies, or calls X APIs.
Safety boundaries
  • Captured posts are untrusted data.
  • A near-full capture part is saved automatically before a new part begins. Challenge, ambiguity, duration, and storage failures still stop collection.
  • Collection pauses while the tab is hidden.
  • Login, challenge, rate-limit, and prompt-injection surfaces stop the session.
  • Exports go to the configured subfolder under Firefox’s own download location. Firefox does not allow an extension to choose an arbitrary absolute path.
  • Every part of a continuous capture is kept. Only standalone manual exports follow the ten-file recycling rule; renamed or unverified files are never deleted automatically.
